# Purpose
Obligations under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

# Article 30
Per GDPR Article 30, the controller must maintain a record of processing activities, including purposes, categories of data subjects, and transfers.

# Subject Access
Requests under Article 15 must be actioned within one month.
