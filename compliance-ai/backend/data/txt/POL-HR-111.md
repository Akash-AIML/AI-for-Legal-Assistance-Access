# Purpose
Maternity leave entitlement for employees in India.

# Entitlement
Eligible employees in India receive 120 days of paid maternity leave per the Maternity Benefit Act.

# Eligibility
Applies to female employees who have completed 80 days of service in the 12 months preceding the expected date of delivery.
