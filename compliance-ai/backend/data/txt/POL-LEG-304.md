# Purpose
Establishes record-keeping requirements to support Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) compliance.

# SOX Section 302
Per SOX Section 302, financial records must be retained for a minimum of 7 years.

# 7.2 Retention Schedule
Section 7.2 details the retention schedule: financial statements 7 years, audit workpapers 7 years, HR records 5 years.
