# Purpose
This policy governs annual leave for employees employed in Germany.

# Entitlement
Employees in Germany receive a statutory entitlement of 30 calendar days of annual leave per year.

# Carry Forward
Unused leave in Germany may be carried forward into the following year up to the full statutory entitlement, and must be used by March 31 of the following year.
