# Purpose
This policy governs annual leave entitlement and carry-forward for employees in India.

# Scope
Applies to all permanent employees employed in India.

# Eligibility
Permanent employees who have completed twelve months of continuous service are entitled to annual leave on a pro-rata basis.

# Carry Forward
## 4.1 General Rule
Employees in India may carry forward up to 10 days of unused annual leave into the following calendar year.

## 4.2 Excess Carry Forward
Any amount above 10 days requires written approval by the employee's manager and confirmation by People Ops.

# Approval
Approval requests flow through the manager, then People Ops, before being recorded in the HR system.
