# Purpose
Outlines how leave benefits differ by employment type in India.

# Permanent Employees
Permanent employees are eligible for annual leave carry-forward as described in the India Annual Leave Policy.

# Contract Employees
Contractors and fixed-term employees are not eligible for annual leave carry-forward and instead receive a pay-in-lieu option.
