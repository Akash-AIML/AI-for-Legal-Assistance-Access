Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At LegalLens Corporation, we take your privacy and the security of your enterprise data extremely seriously. Because our product is designed to interact with sensitive legal, compliance, and human resources documents, our privacy architecture is built around the principles of data minimization, zero-retention (where applicable), and strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).

1. Information We Collect

When you use our services, we collect information that you explicitly provide to us, such as account registration details, billing information, and the organizational documents you upload for vectorization. We also collect automated telemetry data, including IP addresses, browser types, and usage metrics, to ensure the reliable operation of our cloud services and to monitor for security anomalies.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your uploaded documents are processed exclusively to provide the LegalLens AI service. When documents are chunked and embedded using our NVIDIA NIM integrations, the resulting vectors are stored securely in your dedicated ChromaDB tenant. We do not use your proprietary legal or compliance documents to train our foundational language models. Your data remains yours.

3. Data Sharing and Subprocessors

We do not sell your personal or corporate data to third parties. We may share data with trusted subprocessors (such as Microsoft Azure for cloud hosting and Vercel for edge delivery) strictly for the purpose of providing the LegalLens service. All subprocessors are bound by stringent data processing agreements (DPAs) that comply with GDPR, CCPA, and SOC2 standards.

4. Security Measures

We employ enterprise-grade security measures to protect your information. This includes AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. Furthermore, our retrieval architecture applies RBAC filtering at the database level before any data is sent to the LLM, ensuring that unauthorized users cannot access restricted documents through prompt injection attacks.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data handling practices, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@legallens.ai or write to us at our corporate headquarters in San Francisco, CA.
