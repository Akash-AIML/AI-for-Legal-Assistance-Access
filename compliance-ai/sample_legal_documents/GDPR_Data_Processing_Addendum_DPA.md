# Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

This Data Processing Addendum (DPA) supplements the SaaS Service Agreement between DataController Corp (Controller) and CloudData Systems Ltd (Processor).

## 1. Scope and Compliance
Processor shall process Personal Data solely on documented instructions from Controller and in full compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

## 2. Sub-processors
Processor shall not engage any sub-processor without prior written authorization from Controller. Processor remains fully liable for sub-processor performance.

## 3. Data Breach Notification
Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data breach.

## 4. International Data Transfers
Any transfer of Personal Data outside the European Economic Area (EEA) shall be governed by European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

## 5. Audit Rights & Certifications
Processor shall make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance and permit Controller or an independent auditor to conduct annual audits.
