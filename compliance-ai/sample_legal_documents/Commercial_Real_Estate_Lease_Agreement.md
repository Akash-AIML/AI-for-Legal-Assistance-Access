# Commercial Real Estate Lease Agreement

This Commercial Lease Agreement (Lease) is entered into as of April 1, 2026, between Apex Realty Pvt. Ltd. (Landlord) and Nova Analytics Solutions (Tenant).

## 1. Premises & Lease Term
Landlord leases to Tenant Commercial Unit 402, Spectrum Towers, BKC, Mumbai 400051 for a term of thirty-six (36) months commencing May 1, 2026.

## 2. Rent Escalation & Payment
Tenant shall pay monthly base rent of INR 3,50,000 (Three Lakh Fifty Thousand Rupees). Base rent shall escalate by five percent (5%) per annum on each anniversary of the Lease Commencement Date.

## 3. Security Deposit
Tenant shall deposit with Landlord an amount equal to three (3) months' base rent (INR 10,50,000) as Security Deposit for performance of obligations.

## 4. Maintenance & Repairs
Tenant shall be responsible for routine internal repairs and utilities. Landlord shall maintain structural components, elevators, and central HVAC systems.

## 5. Force Majeure & Insurance
Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure caused by acts of God, fire, flood, or government order beyond reasonable control.
